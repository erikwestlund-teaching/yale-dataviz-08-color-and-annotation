# State Population Ranks

Source: U.S. Census 2020 state population counts.

Unit of observation: one state or the District of Columbia.

Teaching use: ranked dot plots, highlighting a few states with color, and grouping states by region.

## Variables

- `state`: two-letter state abbreviation.
- `state_name`: full state name.
- `population`: 2020 census population.
- `rank`: population rank, where 1 is the largest.
